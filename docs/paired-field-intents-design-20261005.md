Publish this plan and its Go source before optimization. The preceding v1 pilot
learned three normal requirements and failed the actual opposite transformations
at first budget. This pilot teaches the alternatives' actual meanings and their
combinations while keeping the same 768/24/8 network and source feature ABI.

mask flips the bit at that field's **source choice position**. Do not confuse
